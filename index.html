Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about my services and working process.

What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in Flutter for mobile development, IoT technologies (ESP32, Raspberry Pi, LoRa), and embedded systems programming. I also have experience with backend technologies like Node.js, MongoDB, and Firebase.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. A simple mobile app might take 4-6 weeks, while a complete IoT solution with custom hardware could take 3-6 months. I provide detailed timelines after understanding your specific requirements.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, I regularly work with clients from around the world. I'm comfortable with remote collaboration and can accommodate different time zones as needed.

What's your development process?

I follow an agile development process with regular updates and iterations. We start with requirements gathering, move to prototyping, then development with frequent testing, and finally deployment with ongoing support.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, I offer maintenance packages that include updates, bug fixes, and feature enhancements. We can discuss a maintenance plan that fits your needs.
