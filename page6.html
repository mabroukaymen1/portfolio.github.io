LoRa-based Environmental Monitoring System

A low-power long-range IoT solution for temperature and humidity monitoring

Project Overview

The LoRaMétéo Smart project, developed at Institut Supérieur d'Informatique de Mahdia (ISIMA) under the supervision of Dr. Zouhour Radaoui, is an IoT solution utilizing LoRa technology for environmental monitoring. It employs Heltec LoRa32 V3 modules and a DHT11 sensor to collect and transmit temperature and humidity data over long distances with minimal power consumption. The system achieved a successful synchronize with a 2% packet loss rate, a range of 300 meters in urban environments, and a power consumption of 0.5 mA during transmission.

Methodology

System architecture and implementation details

System Architecture

The system comprises two main components: an emitter and a receiver. The emitter, equipped with a DHT11 sensor, collects temperature and humidity data and transmits it via LoRa. The receiver captures the data and displays it on an OLED screen. The architecture ensures reliable, long-range communication with low power consumption.

Implementation

The project utilized Arduino IDE for programming the Heltec LoRa32 V3 boards, with DHT.h and heltec-unofficial.h libraries facilitating sensor interfacing and LoRa communication. Lucidchart was used for UML design, ensuring a structured approach to system development. Challenges such as electromagnetic interference were mitigated by increasing transmission power and using a high-quality antenna.

Results and Performance

Key outcomes and performance metrics

Results

The system achieved successful bidirectional communication between two LoRa32 boards, with reliable transmission of temperature and humidity data. Real-time data visualization was accomplished on an OLED display, providing immediate feedback on environmental conditions.

Performance Metrics

- Packet Loss Rate: 2%
- Range: 300 meters in urban environments
- Power Consumption: 0.5 mA during transmission

Applications of LoRa

Real-world use cases for LoRa technology

Why LoRa?

LoRa (Long Range) is a wireless communication technology designed for low-power, long-distance networks, making it ideal for IoT applications requiring extended range and minimal energy consumption.

Application Areas

- Smart Agriculture: Monitoring soil moisture and weather conditions.
- Smart Cities: Managing public lighting and parking occupancy detection.
- Logistics and Transport: Asset tracking and fleet management.
- Environmental Monitoring: Air quality surveillance, water level monitoring, and forest fire detection.
