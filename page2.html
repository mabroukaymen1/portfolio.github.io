Baby Track

A comprehensive Flutter app to support parents and healthcare staff in monitoring newborn health

Project Overview

Baby Track is a Flutter-based mobile application designed to empower parents and healthcare professionals in Tunisia and beyond to monitor and support newborn well-being. The app offers a user-friendly, multilingual interface for tracking breastfeeding sessions, vaccination schedules, pain scores, and more, ensuring comprehensive care for newborns. With Firebase integration for secure data storage and real-time notifications, Baby Track simplifies health management while providing educational resources and instant support through a chatbot.

Technologies Used

Flutter Dart Firebase Provider NIPS Scale PDF Rendering

Breastfeeding Tracking

Log and monitor breastfeeding sessions with detailed tracking of left/right durations, set schedules, and receive timely reminders to ensure consistent feeding.

Vaccination Schedule

Automatically generates the Tunisian vaccination calendar, tracks completed vaccines, and sends notifications for upcoming vaccinations.

Pain Score Monitoring

Record and visualize pain scores using the NIPS scale post-vaccinations or procedures, aiding healthcare staff in assessing newborn comfort.

Medical Library

Access a curated collection of pediatric and breastfeeding resources, with PDF previews and sharing options for easy reference.

Chat Support

Integrated chatbot provides instant answers to common breastfeeding and vaccination questions, offering 24/7 support.

Multilingual Support

Switch between English, French, and Arabic to make the app accessible to a diverse user base in Tunisia and beyond.

Impact & Benefits

Enhanced Newborn Care

Empowers parents with tools to monitor feeding and vaccinations, ensuring timely and consistent care for newborns.

Healthcare Support

Provides healthcare staff with accurate pain score data and vaccination tracking to improve clinical decisions.

Accessibility

Multilingual support and intuitive design make the app accessible to diverse users, including those in rural areas.

Educational Resources

Offers a medical library to educate parents, reducing anxiety and improving health literacy.
