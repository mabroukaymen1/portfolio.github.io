Port Service Management System

A comprehensive Flutter app for streamlining port operations, equipment maintenance, and employee management

Project Overview

The Port Service Management System is a robust Flutter-based mobile application designed to optimize port operations, equipment maintenance, and employee workflows. Leveraging Firebase for real-time data synchronization, secure authentication, and push notifications, the app enables port managers, technicians, and employees to collaborate efficiently. With features like role-based access, real-time tracking, and detailed analytics, it ensures smooth port operations and proactive maintenance management.

Technologies Used

Flutter Dart Firebase Provider Firestore Cloud Messaging

Port Operations Management

Track and manage port operations in real-time, including ship and container management, operation assignments, and status updates.

Equipment Management

Monitor equipment status, schedule maintenance, report failures, and track repairs with a comprehensive inventory system.

Employee Management

Manage employee profiles, assign tasks, track attendance, and handle leave requests with role-based access control.

Notification System

Receive real-time push notifications for equipment breakdowns, maintenance alerts, and operation assignments.

Reporting and Analytics

Generate detailed reports on equipment utilization, maintenance statistics, and operation performance for data-driven decisions.

Impact & Benefits

Efficient Operations

Streamlines port operations with real-time tracking and task assignments, reducing downtime and improving throughput.

Proactive Maintenance

Enables timely equipment maintenance and failure reporting, minimizing disruptions and extending equipment lifespan.

Improved Workforce Management

Enhances employee productivity through efficient task allocation, leave management, and attendance tracking.

Data-Driven Decisions

Provides actionable insights through detailed analytics and reports, optimizing port performance.
